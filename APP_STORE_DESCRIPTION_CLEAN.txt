Keep Your Pet's Health in One Place

PawsLog is the simple, beautiful way to track and manage your pet's health and wellness. Whether you have cats, dogs, or multiple pets, PawsLog helps you stay organized and informed about your furry family members' wellbeing.

COMPREHENSIVE HEALTH TRACKING

- Weight Monitoring: Track your pet's weight over time with detailed records. Monitor growth, weight loss, or maintenance goals with easy-to-read history.

- Feeding Records: Log every meal with food type, quantity, and time. Set daily feeding limits to ensure your pets maintain healthy eating habits.

- Medical History: Keep unlimited medical records including vaccinations, check-ups, medications, and treatments. Never forget an important appointment or medication schedule.

SMART ORGANIZATION

- Pet Groups: Organize your pets into custom groups (e.g., Indoor Pets, Outdoor Pets, Young Pets) for easy management.

- Tags System: Add custom tags to your pets for quick filtering and organization. Find pets instantly by their tags.

- Pet Profiles: Create detailed profiles for each pet with custom icons, descriptions, and information.

BEAUTIFUL & INTUITIVE DESIGN

- Clean, modern interface inspired by Bootstrap design principles
- Smooth animations and transitions for a comfortable user experience
- Compact, organized layout that makes information easy to find
- Responsive design that works perfectly on all iOS devices

DATA MANAGEMENT

- Export & Import: Backup and restore your pet data using YAML format. Share data between devices or keep secure backups.

- Local Storage: All your data is stored securely on your device. Your pet's information stays private and accessible offline.

- Unlimited Records: Track as many weight, feeding, and medical records as you need. No arbitrary limits.

WHY PAWSLOG

PawsLog was designed with simplicity and privacy in mind. Unlike other pet tracking apps, your data never leaves your device. No accounts, no cloud sync, no subscriptions - just a powerful, easy-to-use tool for responsible pet care.
